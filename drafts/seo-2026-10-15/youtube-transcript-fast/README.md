# YouTube Transcript & Subtitles Scraper — Captions, SRT, VTT

YouTube Transcript Scraper gets the full transcript of any YouTube video, playlist or channel: every line with its timestamp, the whole text in one field, and SRT or WebVTT subtitle files if you want them. No browser is opened, so it is fast: in a test run fifteen videos were processed in nine seconds. Videos without captions, unavailable videos, bad links and failed fetches are reported in the dataset and cost nothing.

## What data does YouTube Transcript Scraper extract?

For every video:

- `videoId`, `url`, `title`, `channel`, `channelId`, `lengthSeconds`, `viewCount` – the video
- `status` – `OK`, `NO_CAPTIONS` or `FAILED`
- `text` – the whole transcript as one string, ready for an LLM, a summary or a search index
- `segments`, `segmentCount` – every line with `start` and `duration` in seconds
- `srt`, `vtt` – the transcript as a subtitle file, when you ask for it
- `language` – the track you received, e.g. `en`, `de-DE`
- `isAutoGenerated` – `true` for YouTube's automatic captions
- `languageMatched` – `false` if your language was missing and another track was returned
- `availableLanguages` – every caption track the video has
- `translationFailed` – with machine translation on, when YouTube refused it
- `source` – the playlist or channel link the video came from
- `error` – why a video has no transcript

A link that is not a YouTube video, playlist or channel gets its own `INVALID_INPUT` record, so you can see what was skipped.

## How to use YouTube Transcript Scraper

1. Open the Actor page and press **Try for free**. The Input tab is prefilled with a working example, so one click starts a run.
2. Paste links into **YouTube links**: regular video links, `youtu.be` short links, Shorts, embeds, live links, playlist links, channel links (`@handle` or `/channel/…`), or bare video ids.
3. For playlists and channels, set **Max videos per playlist or channel** (default 50, up to 1,000). A channel gives you its newest uploads first.
4. Optionally set **Language** (default `en`) and tick **SRT** or **WebVTT**. A human-written track is chosen over an auto-generated one; if your language is missing you get the video's best track, and the output says so.
5. Press **Start** and download the results from the **Output** tab.

## How much does it cost?

**$0.001 per transcript delivered, $1.00 per 1,000 transcripts.** Videos without captions are free, and so are failed videos, bad links and playlist or channel listings. If your run has a **Maximum charge**, the Actor stops cleanly when it is reached, delivers what fits and tells you how many videos were left.

Worked example: fifteen videos in nine seconds cost **$0.015**. A channel's newest 1,000 uploads, the maximum per channel, cost at most $1.00, and less when some videos have no captions.

## Is it legal to scrape YouTube transcripts?

The Actor reads caption tracks that YouTube serves publicly for a video, plus the video's public title, channel name, length and view count. It collects no personal contact data: no phone numbers, e-mail addresses or private profiles, so there is nothing to strip under GDPR. Transcripts are the creator's content; use them within copyright law and YouTube's terms. Private and age-restricted videos are reported as `FAILED` with YouTube's own reason. You remain responsible for how you use the data. This is an independent tool, not affiliated with YouTube.

## FAQ

**A video came back as `NO_CAPTIONS`.** The video has no captions and no automatic subtitles on YouTube, so there is nothing to download. You were not charged for it.

**I asked for Hungarian but got English.** The video has no Hungarian track. You get its best available track, a human-written English one if there is one, with `languageMatched: false`, and `availableLanguages` lists every language the video does have.

**Are automatic captions included?** Yes, when a video has no human-written track. `isAutoGenerated` tells you which one you got.

**Can it machine-translate?** Optionally, best effort. YouTube limits this heavily: in a test it came through for 1 of 8 videos. When it does not, you still receive the original transcript, marked `translationFailed: true`, at the normal price. It is off by default because each refusal is retried and runs get slower.

**How many videos from a channel?** Up to 1,000 per channel or playlist, set by **Max videos per playlist or channel**. The newest uploads come first.
